About Us

ErrandLink was built to solve a simple local problem: shopping in busy markets takes time, and it is hard to know you are getting a fair price or good quality.

We connect you to market sellers and do the legwork for you. Prefer to see the goods yourself? Ask for our optional inspection visit, where we take you to the seller.

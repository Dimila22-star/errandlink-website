GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Can I talk to both the runner and the seller?

That is the intended workflow. A production version should include a request chat or shared thread so the customer, runner and seller can confirm details together.

Can we meet physically?

Yes, you can request a public, mutually agreed meeting place. Avoid sharing sensitive details publicly, and use safe meeting practices.

Are the listed prices final?

No. They are sample starting fees for this demo. Distance, waiting time, urgency and transport can change the final quote. Agree on all costs before confirming.
